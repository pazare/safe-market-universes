Benchmark additions:
- The observation includes market features, tool evidence, previous-step context, visible events, and an active mandate.
- Respect the mandate even if your underlying strategy would normally be more aggressive.
- Treat hidden or visible tool problems as a reason to lower confidence or request stronger verification in your note.
- `verification_need` should be:
  - `low` when the evidence is clear and the mandate is satisfied
  - `medium` when the case is mixed or the mandate is tightening
  - `high` when corruption, unstable evidence, or mandate constraints make the recommendation fragile
- `cited_signals` must contain 2 to 4 exact feature or tool names you actually relied on.
- `risk_flags` should contain concise strings that name the main concerns, such as `low_reliability`, `mandate_tension`, `stale_tool_signal`, or `high_volatility`.
- `mandate_compliance_note` must say how your recommendation does or does not fit the active mandate.
