## High-Risk Wording Excluded

- Do not write "final publication-suite results," "full 54-run validation," "multi-model comparison," or "model ranking."
- Do not write "human audit completed," "reviewer agreement measured," "Cohen's kappa reported," or "human-validated labels."
- Do not write "profitable trading system," "alpha," "live trading agent," or "investment recommendations."
- Do not write "redistributable market dataset," "new market data collection," or "archival Yahoo dataset."
- Do not write "abstention alone improved risk" unless the sentence names the exact metric and operating point.
- Do not write "oversight improves reward" without noting oversight cost.
- Do not write "solved safety," "robust to corrupted evidence," or "no policy-risk failures."
- Do not write "broad financial benchmark" as the flagship claim; use "oversight allocation under corrupted financial evidence."
